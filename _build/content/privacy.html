Privacy

The short version: the free tools keep everything in your own browser. The only thing we collect is your email address, and only if you join the waiting list.

Who we are

The Alignment Plan is run by Calco Studios Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17439973). Its registered office is 4 Kiln Drive, Tydd St Mary, Wisbech, Cambridgeshire, PE13 5RA. We decide how your information is used. You can reach us at {{EMAIL}}.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you join the waiting list. Nothing else is asked for.
  • Any email you send us. We keep it so we can reply.

What we do not collect

  • Your answers and scores. The wheel of life, reflection questions, goals template, vision board, bingo card, planner, journal prompts and life audit all save to your own browser. They are never sent to us and we cannot see them. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Your photos. Photos you add to a vision board stay on your device.
  • Tracking. There are no advertising cookies and no analytics on this site.

Why we use your email address

To tell you when The Alignment Plan opens, and to send news about it. We do this because you asked us to when you joined the list. You can change your mind at any time: every email has an unsubscribe link, or you can write to us.

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site and Cloudflare routes its address and our email. Like any host, they see technical details such as your IP address when your browser loads a page.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser asks Google for the font files.

We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If The Alignment Plan closes, the list is deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent. Write to {{EMAIL}} and we will reply within a month. If you are unhappy with how we have handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
