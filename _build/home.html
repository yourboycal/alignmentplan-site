How The Alignment Plan works

The tools on this page are the free part. The Alignment Plan is what joins them up and keeps them going after January.

  1. Reflect. A deep session in December to understand where you are and what is missing.
  2. Plan. One focus for the year and up to three big goals, each broken down into a step for every month.
  3. Stay aligned. One tap every Friday and ten minutes on the first of the month, so the plan is still alive in June.

Most year-end reviews stop on 31 December. This one is built for the other eleven months.

Start with one of these

  • Five minutes? Do the wheel of life and see which areas need attention.
  • Fifteen minutes? Make a vision board with your own photos, or do a life audit.
  • An hour? Work through the end of year reflection questions.
  • Know what you want already? Go straight to the yearly goals template or fill in a 2027 bingo card.
  • Planning the year? Print the 2027 year planner.
