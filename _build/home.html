Features

What's inside The Alignment Plan.

Five things, built to work together: a reflection, a plan, a weekly nudge, a wheel that shows what's moving, and a number that tells you if your week matched your goals.

Reflect

A deep session to see where you're at, and what's missing.

This is a proper sit-down, not a quick check-in. You take the long view first, then the last twelve months, then an honest look at today. What gave you energy? What did you say yes to that you should have turned down? By the end you can see where you really are, what's working, and what's missing.

  • Three parts: the long view, the last twelve months, today
  • Your answers save as you write, so you can stop and come back
  • Eight areas of your life rated 1 to 10, so the gaps show

Plan

Big goals, broken down month by month.

Choose one focus for the year and up to three big goals. Then work backwards from each finish line: what has to be true by June, by March, by the end of January? You leave with a step for every month, and only this month's step asks anything of you.

  • One focus for the year, so you know what to say no to
  • Each big goal split into a step for every month
  • Every month gets its own focus, taken straight from the plan

Stay aligned

A nudge, not a nag. One tap a week, ten minutes a month.

Every Friday you get one question about this month's step: did you move on it? Yes, a bit, or no. One tap, thirty seconds, nothing to write. On the first of the month you get the proper check-in: re-rate the eight areas, then confirm next month's step from your plan, or change it. Your focus for the year and this month's step sit at the top of both, so the small stuff stays tied to the big picture.

  • Four Friday taps roll into the monthly check-in, so you never start from a blank
  • The wheel shows this month against December
  • In June, your December answers come back for a second read

Does your week match your goals?

Connect your AI agents to the Alignment Plan and they keep watch on two things: your direction and where your time goes. Each week they read your calendar and tasks, sort them against your goals, and tell you how much of your time went where you said it mattered. Nothing to log.

  • One number a week: the share of your time that went on your goals
  • Once connected, it arrives with your Friday question
  • Ask where you stand on any goal, or hand a step to your agents

Planned as a connector for Claude, ChatGPT and other assistants that support them. Your calendar stays with your assistant. The Alignment Plan only keeps the weekly totals.
