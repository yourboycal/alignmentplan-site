What is a goals bingo card?

A goals bingo card is a grid of things you want to do in a year, one in each square. When you do one, you cross it off. A full row, column or diagonal is a bingo. Every square crossed off is a full house.

The idea spread on social media in the last couple of new years as a friendlier way to set goals. A list of resolutions is either kept or broken. A bingo card gives you credit for each square, and more than one way to win.

How to make a 2027 bingo card

  1. Pick a size. Five by five is the classic card and gives you 25 squares. Four by four is 16. Three by three is nine, and a good choice if 25 sounds like a lot.
  2. Write a goal in each square. Keep each one short and finishable. "Read 12 books" works. "Read more" does not, because you can never cross it off.
  3. Mix the sizes. A few big ones, a lot of middling ones and a handful you could do this weekend. Easy squares get the card moving.
  4. Use the middle as a free square. On a five by five card the centre is already crossed off for you. Change it if you would rather earn it.
  5. Shuffle. Press "Shuffle" so the hard goals are not all in one row.
  6. Save it or print it. Press "Save as image" to share it or keep it on your phone. Press "Print my card" for the fridge.

Five rules for a card you will finish

  1. Only write things you control. "Apply for five jobs" is yours to do. "Get promoted" depends on someone else.
  2. Make every square a yes or no. You should be able to say, without arguing with yourself, whether it is done.
  3. Cover more than one part of your life. A card that is all work or all fitness gets dull by March. The wheel of life shows which areas need the most attention.
  4. Aim for one line by Easter. An early bingo makes the rest of the card feel possible.
  5. Look at it on the first of every month. Cross off what you have done and pick one square for the month ahead.

Bingo card or goals list?

A bingo card is good at breadth. It gets you doing a lot of different things, and it is forgiving. Miss five squares and you can still finish the year with several lines.

It is less good at the one big thing. If you have a goal that will take all year, such as changing career or clearing a debt, a single square does not do it justice. Put that goal in the yearly goals template, where it gets a step for every month, and keep the card for everything else.

Questions about goals bingo cards

What is a 2027 bingo card?

It is a grid of goals for the year, one in each square, usually five by five. You cross a square off when you do it. A full row, column or diagonal is a bingo. People use it as a lighter alternative to a list of new year resolutions.

How many squares should a goals bingo card have?

Twenty-five is the classic, in a five by five grid with a free square in the middle. If that feels like too many, use four by four for 16 goals or three by three for nine. A small card you finish beats a big one you abandon.

What should I put on my bingo card?

Short goals that you control and can finish, spread across different parts of your life: work, money, health, people, fun and home. Mix a few big ones with plenty of small ones. This page lists 64 ideas to start from.

Is this bingo card generator free?

Yes. It is free, needs no sign-up and works in your browser. You can fill the card with ideas, change any square, save it as an image and print it.

Can I print a blank bingo card template?

Yes. Press Print a blank card for an empty grid with your title at the top. Choose Save as PDF in the print window if you want to keep it as a file or send it to someone.

How do I share my bingo card?

Press Save as image. On a phone you can then send it straight to a chat or save it to your photos. On a computer it downloads as a picture you can post or send.

Where is my card saved?

In your own browser, on this device. Nothing is sent to us. Your ticks are saved too, so you can come back during the year and cross squares off. Clearing your browsing data removes it, so save an image as a backup.
