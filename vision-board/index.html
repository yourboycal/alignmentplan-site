What is a vision board?

A vision board is a single page of pictures and words that shows the life you are working towards. You choose an image or a phrase for each thing you want, put them together, and keep the result where you will see it every day.

People used to make them with magazines, scissors and glue. Most are now made on a screen and kept as a phone wallpaper. The point is the same. It is a daily reminder of what you decided mattered, on a day when you had time to think about it.

How to make a vision board in six steps

  1. Decide what you want before you look for pictures. Start with words. If you begin with images, you end up with a board of things that look nice. The wheel of life takes five minutes and shows you which areas of your life need the most attention.
  2. Give each area of your life one square. The maker above starts with eight: career, money, health, family and friends, love, personal growth, fun and home. Rename any that do not fit you.
  3. Write a few words in each square. Be specific. "Run 5k without stopping" is better than "get fit", because you will know when it has happened.
  4. Add a photo where you have a good one. Your own photos work best: the place, the person, the house, the finish line. A stock picture of a stranger on a beach is easy to ignore.
  5. Put the year in the middle, and one word for it. The word is your focus. It is the thing you want the whole year to be about.
  6. Save it and put it where you will see it. Press "Save as image" for a picture, or switch the shape to "Phone wallpaper" first. Press "Print my board" for the fridge or the wall by your desk.

What to put on a vision board: ideas for each area

Stuck on a square? These are examples to adapt, not a list to copy. Your board should sound like you.

CareerA job title you want. A client you would love to win. Fridays off. The words "my own business".
MoneyA number in savings. A debt at zero. A pension you understand. One generous thing you want to afford.
HealthA distance, a lift or a class. Eight hours of sleep. A photo of you at a time you felt strong.
Family and friendsA standing Sunday lunch. A trip with your oldest friend. A photo of the people you want to see more.
LoveA weekend away. A weekly evening with no phones. What a good relationship feels like, in three words.
Personal growthA language, an instrument, a course. Twelve books. One thing that scares you a little.
FunA festival, a hobby you dropped, a sport you used to play. Something with no purpose except that you like it.
HomeA room you want to finish. A garden. A street you want to live on. A table big enough for everyone.

Do vision boards work? What the research says

A vision board on its own will not change much, and it is worth being honest about that. Two studies explain why, and what to do about it.

The first looked at students before an exam. Those who spent a few minutes each day picturing themselves studying went on to study more and get better grades. Picturing the good grade itself did not bring the same gain. Imagining the work helped more than imagining the result.1

The second found that daydreaming about an ideal future can leave you with less energy to chase it. Across four experiments, people who imagined everything going perfectly were less energised afterwards than people who pictured the future more critically. A lovely daydream can feel like the job is already done.2

Neither study tested vision boards directly. But together they point the same way. Pictures of the result are a start. What moves you is knowing the next step.

How to make a vision board that actually works

  1. Give every square a first step. Under "Run 5k without stopping", the step might be "walk for twenty minutes on Monday". The yearly goals template breaks a goal into a step for every month.
  2. Name what will get in the way. For each square, ask what usually stops you. Then decide now what you will do when it happens.
  3. Keep it in sight. A board in a drawer does nothing. A lock screen gets looked at dozens of times a day.
  4. Check it on the first of the month. Ask one question of each square: did I do anything about this? Ten minutes is enough.
  5. Change it when you change. If a square stops mattering, replace it. The board should describe what you want, not what you wanted in January.

How to make a vision board on your phone

Open this page on your phone and fill in the squares. Tap "Add photo" to choose pictures from your camera roll. Then set the shape to "Phone wallpaper" and press "Save as image". Choose "Save Image" to put it in your photos.

The wallpaper shape leaves space at the top for the clock and at the bottom for the buttons on your lock screen. Set it from your photos app in the usual way.

A vision board template to print

If you would rather use scissors and glue, press "Print a blank template". You get nine empty squares with your eight area names and the year in the middle, ready for magazine cuttings and a pen. Choose "Save as PDF" in the print window if you want a file.

"Print my board" prints the board as you have made it, photos included.

Questions about vision boards

How do I make a vision board online for free?

Use the maker at the top of this page. Type a few words into each of the eight squares, add your own photos where you have them, and put the year and one word in the middle. Then press Save as image. It is free and needs no sign-up.

What should I put on my vision board?

One thing you want for each area of your life: career, money, health, family and friends, love, personal growth, fun and home. Be specific. A distance to run, a number to save or a place to visit works better than a general wish, because you will know when it has happened.

Do vision boards actually work?

Not on their own. Research suggests that picturing a result, without picturing the work, does little and can even reduce the energy you put in. A vision board helps when each picture is paired with a next step and you look at the board often.

How do I make a vision board on my phone?

Open this page on your phone, fill in the squares and add photos from your camera roll. Set the shape to Phone wallpaper, press Save as image and choose Save Image. Then set it as your lock screen from your photos app.

Is there a vision board template I can print?

Yes. Press Print a blank template to get nine empty squares with your area names, ready for cuttings and a pen. Choose Save as PDF in the print window to keep it as a file. Print my board prints your finished board.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Your photos are shrunk and stored in your own browser on this device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing your browsing data removes them, so save your board as an image if you want to keep it.

When is the best time to make a vision board?

Any time you have a quiet hour. Late December and early January are popular because a new year is a natural point to take stock. It works just as well on a birthday, after a big change, or on an ordinary Sunday.
